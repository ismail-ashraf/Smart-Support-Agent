\# Smile Clinic - Post-Treatment Instructions



\## Instructions After Tooth Extraction

\- Keep the gauze pad over the extraction site with firm pressure for 45-60 minutes.

\- Do not spit, rinse vigorously, or drink through a straw for the first 24 hours.

\- Avoid hot food and hot beverages for 24 hours; stick to cold and soft foods (ice cream, yogurt, cold soup).

\- Avoid smoking for at least 48 hours to prevent dry socket infection.

\- Take prescribed pain relievers and antibiotics strictly as directed by your doctor.



\## Instructions After Teeth Whitening

\- Avoid dark-colored drinks and food (coffee, tea, cola, soy sauce, red sauce) for 48 hours ("White Diet").

\- Mild tooth sensitivity is normal for 24-48 hours. Use desensitizing toothpaste if needed.
