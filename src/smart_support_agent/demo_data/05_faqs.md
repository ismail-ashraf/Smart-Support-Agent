\# Smile Clinic - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)



Q: Is teeth cleaning painful?

A: No, professional scaling and polishing is completely painless. You may only feel mild vibrations and sensitivity if you have gum inflammation.



Q: How long does a dental implant procedure take?

A: Placing the implant takes about 30 to 45 minutes under local anesthesia. Full integration and crown placement take 2 to 4 months.



Q: Do you offer free consultations?

A: Initial examinations cost 300 EGP, but the consultation fee is deducted from your total treatment cost if you start treatment on the same day.



Q: Is teeth whitening safe for enamel?

A: Yes, our professionally supervised whitening systems do not damage enamel when instructions are properly followed.
