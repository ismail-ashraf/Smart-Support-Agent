\# Smile Clinic - Booking \& Cancellation Policy



\## Reservation Policy

\- All appointments must be booked at least 24 hours in advance via Telegram bot or phone call.

\- Emergency cases are accepted without prior booking based on clinic availability.



\## Rescheduling \& Cancellation

\- Free cancellation or rescheduling is allowed up to 12 hours before the scheduled appointment.

\- Late cancellations (less than 12 hours) or no-shows will incur a 150 EGP fee on the next visit.



\## Payment Methods

\- We accept Cash, Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard), and Mobile Wallets (Vodafone Cash, InstaPay).

\- Installment plans are available via ValU and Premium Card for transactions above 3,000 EGP.
